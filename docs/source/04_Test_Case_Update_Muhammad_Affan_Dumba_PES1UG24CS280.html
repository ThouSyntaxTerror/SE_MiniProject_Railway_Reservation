1. Purpose and Execution Rules

This update specifies ten cases that cover the RRS functional, non-functional and security requirements. It is controlled as Test Plan §5.2 and supplied separately to identify the Deliverable 4 owner.

  • Use synthetic P1/P2 passenger accounts and A1 administrator; never use real passenger/payment data.
  • Reset independent cases to a known seed. Record build, environment, tester, date, actual result, evidence and status.
  • A case passes only when every expected result is observed. Redact passwords and tokens from evidence.

2. Test Cases

TC-01 — Registration, Password Boundary and Credential Storage

PreconditionsNo P1 account; authorized test-only storage/log access.
StepsSubmit 11-character password; then valid 12-character password and unique email; repeat email; inspect stored credential, response and logs.
Expected11-character and duplicate inputs are rejected; exactly one account is created; stored value is a salted adaptive hash unequal to the password; plaintext is absent from response/logs.
TraceabilityFR-01; SEC-02

TC-02 — Sign-In, Lockout and Authentication Audit

PreconditionsP1 exists and is unlocked.
StepsSign in correctly and out; submit five wrong passwords within 15 minutes; attempt a sixth; inspect audit; retry valid credentials after 15 minutes.
ExpectedValid sign-in works; errors are generic; sixth attempt is rejected for 15 minutes; authentication outcomes are audited without passwords; valid access resumes after the period.
TraceabilityFR-02, FR-09; SEC-02, SEC-04

TC-03 — Search and Availability

PreconditionsMatching, nonmatching, sold-out and multiple-departure trips seeded.
StepsSearch by origin, destination and date; inspect filtering, ordering and seat counts.
ExpectedOnly exact matches appear in departure order; every count remains from zero through capacity.
TraceabilityFR-03, FR-04

TC-04 — Successful Booking, History and Audit

PreconditionsP1 signed in; selected trip has one free seat; simulator succeeds.
StepsBook once; query P1 history and audit records.
ExpectedOne confirmed booking, unique PNR and one seat allocation; P1 history contains all required fields; confirmation audit contains actor/action/time/outcome.
TraceabilityFR-05, FR-06, FR-09

TC-05 — Payment Failure and Idempotent Retry

PreconditionsP1 signed in; one free seat; known idempotency key.
StepsRun once with simulator failure; then use success and send the same request twice with one new idempotency key.
ExpectedFailure confirms nothing and releases the hold; duplicate successes produce one booking/PNR and allocate one seat.
TraceabilityFR-05; NFR-03

TC-06 — Ownership, Eligible/Late Cancellation and Audit

PreconditionsP1 owns future and departed confirmed bookings; P2 signed in separately.
StepsP2 reads/cancels P1’s future booking; P1 cancels it twice; P1 attempts departed-booking cancellation; inspect inventory/refund/audit.
ExpectedP2 gets 403/404 without details; first eligible cancellation releases one seat with refund and audit; repeated/late attempts cause no additional inventory change.
TraceabilityFR-06, FR-07, FR-09; SEC-01

TC-07 — Administrator Inventory, Authorization and Audit

PreconditionsA1 administrator and P1 passenger exist.
StepsP1 attempts admin create; A1 creates, edits and disables a trip; inspect state/audit.
ExpectedP1 gets 403; A1 changes persist; every change audit has actor, time, action and outcome.
TraceabilityFR-08, FR-09; SEC-01

TC-08 — Concurrent Final Seat

PreconditionsExactly one available seat; 20 authenticated passengers; simulator succeeds.
StepsRelease 20 requests simultaneously from a barrier; inspect bookings and availability.
ExpectedAt most one confirmation; others report unavailable; final count is zero and never negative.
TraceabilityFR-04, FR-05; NFR-03

TC-09 — Transport, Session and Restart Recovery

PreconditionsHTTPS deployment; P1 session; committed booking.
StepsInspect transport; sign out/replay token; wait 30 minutes idle and reuse session; restart app; query health/booking.
ExpectedAuthenticated traffic is HTTPS; signed-out/expired tokens fail; booking persists; health responds within 60 seconds; logs omit passwords/tokens.
TraceabilityNFR-04; SEC-02, SEC-03

TC-10 — Performance, Accessibility and Compatibility

Preconditions10,000 trips; 50 users; current Chrome/Edge/Firefox; 375px/1280px.
StepsRun 200 searches and 100 bookings; complete required workflows by keyboard at both widths in every browser; inspect accessible names.
Expected≥95% searches ≤2s and bookings ≤3s under stated conditions; keyboard works; controls have accessible names; primary forms do not scroll horizontally.
TraceabilityNFR-01, NFR-02, NFR-05, NFR-06
